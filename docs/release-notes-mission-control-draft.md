## English

### AgentTeams Dashboard — Mission Control for multi-agent teams

Managing a fleet of AI agents should feel like mission control, not ticket whack-a-mole. AgentTeams Dashboard gives operators one console to supervise multi-agent teams end to end:

- **See everything** — live cluster topology, Worker/Team health, Matrix room activity and infrastructure status on a single overview.
- **Intervene anywhere** — a task board with human-in-the-loop approvals: HITL confirmation cards surface right where the work happens.
- **Watch agents think** — Matrix chat renders runtime-aware streams (thinking blocks, tool calls, long-message attachments) for every supported runtime, with full replay of past sessions.
- **Diagnose faster** — the WenTian assistant collects logs, sessions and messages into one AI-assisted report with root-cause analysis and remediation steps.
- **Govern by default** — RBAC levels, an append-only audit trail, and PII-redacted debug bundles.

Standalone or embedded, one line to install. Mission control for your multi-agent teams starts here.

## 中文

### AgentTeams Dashboard —— 多智能体团队的 Mission Control

管理一支 AI 智能体舰队，应该像任务控制中心一样从容。AgentTeams Dashboard 让操作者在一个控制台端到端督导多智能体团队：

- **全景可视** —— 集群拓扑、Worker/Team 健康、Matrix 房间动态与基础设施状态，一屏尽览。
- **随处干预** —— 带人工介入（HITL）审批的任务看板：确认卡出现在工作发生的地方。
- **看懂智能体** —— Matrix 聊天按运行时渲染流式过程（思考块、工具调用、长消息附件），历史会话完整回放。
- **更快诊断** —— 问天助手把日志、会话与消息汇成一份 AI 诊断报告：根因分析 + 修复步骤。
- **默认治理** —— RBAC 分级、追加式审计日志、PII 脱敏的调试日志包。

独立部署或一行脚本嵌入，多智能体团队的 Mission Control 从这里开始。
